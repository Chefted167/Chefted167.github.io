QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

What does SONARA MUSIC do?

SONARA MUSIC provides music distribution and artist services designed to help independent creators prepare, release and grow their music.

Can independent artists use SONARA MUSIC?

Yes. The platform is designed with independent artists in mind, whether you are releasing your first single or building a catalogue.

Where can my music be distributed?

Distribution destinations depend on the release and the current SONARA MUSIC distribution network. Contact us for the latest supported platforms.

How do I submit my music?

Use the contact section below and send your artist name, song/release title and the service you need. SONARA MUSIC can then guide you through the next steps.
